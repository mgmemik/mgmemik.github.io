Delete your Fishyork account

Fishyork · thebarnapp

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Fishyork and go to Settings › Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your player account and all game data (tanks, fish, game currency, orders, shows) are deleted from our server immediately and permanently. Backups expire within 30 days. You can first tap Download my data for a copy.

Without the app

Write to app@save-all.com from the e-mail you want us to answer and include your player number (Settings › Account). We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by e-mail.

Anonymous usage statistics (TelemetryDeck) are not linked to you and cannot be tied to an account. See the Privacy Policy.
