Privacy Policy

Fishyork · thebarnapp · Effective 24 November 2026

In short: Fishyork is an aquarium game with an online save. To keep your aquarium we store a random player account and your game progress. We never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, contacts, location, photos or microphone. There are no ads and no tracking. You can download or delete all your data in the app at any time.

Who we are

Fishyork is made by thebarnapp, a sole proprietorship registered in Istanbul, Türkiye. Contact: app@save-all.com.

Data we store on our server

  • Player account: a random player number created on first start, the time and time zone of sign-up (so the game day matches your clock) and the last time the app connected. The device keeps a random secret that signs it in; we store only a one-way hash of the session tokens.
  • Sign in with Apple / Google (optional): if you link your account, we store only the stable account identifier Apple or Google gives the app (not your name or e-mail), so you can restore your aquarium on a new device.
  • Game progress: your tanks, fish, plants, decorations, water readings, game currency (Pul), orders, quests, lessons and the list of game actions you made. These are game data, not personal data about you.
  • Server logs: to keep the service safe (rate limits, errors) the server processes your IP address in memory and in short technical logs kept at most 30 days.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which parts of the game are used, the app sends anonymous signals to TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany): an event name (for example "order filled", "level up"), app version, platform, OS version, language and coarse session counts. The identity is a one-way digest of a random installation number; no name, e-mail, advertising identifier or location is sent and the data is not used for tracking. You can turn it off in Settings.

Notifications

If you allow them, reminders (a birth is due, an order expires) are scheduled on your device. We run no push server and see nothing about them.

Sharing

"Share" opens your device's share sheet with a picture of your tank. The app does not send it itself.

Where data is processed

Our server runs on Google Cloud (europe-west1, Belgium) behind Cloudflare. We do not sell or rent data and share it with no one except these service providers.

Your rights

In the app, Settings › Account: "Download my data" gives you a copy of everything we store, and "Delete account" deletes your player account and all game data from our server immediately and permanently (backups expire within 30 days). You can also write to us. Residents of the EU/EEA, UK and Türkiye (KVKK) have the rights of access, correction, deletion and objection; you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Fishyork is for players aged 13 and over. The app asks for the year of birth before creating an account and does not create one for younger players; the year itself is not stored or sent.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
